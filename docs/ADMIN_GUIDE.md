# Cafe Eleganza - Manager & Staff Operations Guide
## رہنما برائے مینیجر و عملہ (اردو و انگریزی)

---

### 1. Adding a New Menu Item / نیا آئٹم شامل کرنا
**English:**
1. Log in to the Admin Portal by clicking the top right shield icon or navigating to `/admin`.
2. Select the **Menu** tab.
3. Click **Add New Dish / Drink**.
4. Fill in:
   - English Name & Urdu Name (e.g. `Vanilla Bean Frappe` / `ونیلا بین فریپے`)
   - Price in PKR (e.g. `850`)
   - Category (e.g. `Artisan Coffee`, `Wood-Fired Pizza`, `Limka Coolers`)
   - Ingredients, allergens, and description.
5. Click **Save Item**. It will immediately appear on all customer devices and QR table screens.

**اردو:**
ایڈمن پورٹل پر لاگ ان کریں اور 'Menu' ٹیب منتخب کریں۔ 'Add New Dish / Drink' پر کلک کر کے انگریزی و اردو نام اور قیمت درج کریں اور محفوظ کریں۔

---

### 2. Marking an Item "Sold Out" (86 Item) / آئٹم ختم کرنا
**English:**
If ingredients run out (e.g. fresh strawberries or steaks):
1. In the **Menu** tab, find the item.
2. Tap the **Available** badge. It will turn red and show **"86 / Sold Out"**.
3. Customers will instantly see "Sold Out" and won't be able to add it to their carts.

---

### 3. Handling Live Orders & Printing Tickets / آرڈرز اور پرنٹنگ
**English:**
1. When a customer or table places an order, an audible chime sounds in the restaurant tablet.
2. In the **Orders** tab, select the order.
3. Review the customer address, table number, and customized notes.
4. Click **Print Ticket** for the 80mm thermal kitchen slip.
5. Update status: `Pending` → `Preparing` → `Ready` → `Delivered`.

---

### 4. Printing Table QR Cards / ٹیبل کیو آر کوڈز پرنٹ کرنا
1. Open the **QR Tables** tab.
2. Click **Generate Printable QR Cards**.
3. Each table (1 to 14) will display a branded QR card. Click print or export to A4 glossy paper for placement on acrylic table stands.
